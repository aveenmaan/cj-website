About CJ's Liquors

Fremont's Family-Owned
Liquor Store

Since 1998, CJ's Liquors has been a cornerstone of the Fremont community. What started as a small neighborhood shop has grown into the East Bay's most trusted destination for fine wines, premium spirits, and a massive cold beer selection.

We believe in real relationships — with our customers, our suppliers, and our community. Our team personally selects every bottle on our shelves, ensuring you always get the best quality at the fairest prices. Whether you're celebrating a special occasion or just picking up something for the weekend, we're here to help you find exactly what you're looking for.

Family-owned and operated for over 25 years
2,000+ bottles — largest selection in Fremont
Walk-in cooler with hundreds of cold beer options
Knowledgeable staff who know their product
Special orders for rare and hard-to-find bottles
